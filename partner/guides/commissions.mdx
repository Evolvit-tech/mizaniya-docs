Your account sits in the Mizaniya Pay network like any point of sale. What you earn on a sale depends on the kind of product. In every case, **no account can give the level below it better terms than it gets itself**, so you never earn less than zero on a sale.

## Mobile top-ups: a share of the commission

The operator pays a commission on each top-up, as a percentage of the amount. The customer pays the normal face value: the commission is already included in it.

Your rate is set by the account above you, from 0% up to its own rate. On each top-up you sell, you earn your full rate.

**Example.** Your rate is 1% and you sell a 1,000 DZD top-up. You earn 1% × 1,000 = **10 DZD**, which is added to your profit.

<Note>
  If the operator changes its commission, every rate in the network changes in the same proportion, so you keep the same share of it. At a lower operator rate, your 1% could become 0.8%.
</Note>

## Gift cards and eSIMs: cost plus markup

The provider sells each card or eSIM at a set cost price. Each account above you adds its markup, as a fixed amount in DZD or as a percentage of its own cost, and the result is **your cost price**: the `price` you see in the catalog.

You then sell at your own price. What you earn is the difference between your selling price and your cost.

**Example.** The provider sells a card for 2,000 DZD. The admin adds 500 DZD, and the master above you adds 10% (250 DZD). Your cost is **2,750 DZD**. If you sell the card at 3,000 DZD, you earn 250 DZD.

<Note>
  A fixed markup stays the same amount when the provider changes its price. A percentage markup is recalculated on the new cost. In the example, if the provider raises the card to 2,400 DZD, your cost becomes 3,190 DZD.
</Note>

## Where you see your earnings

| Where | What |
| --- | --- |
| `earnedMargin` on each order | What that sale earned your account, in DZD. |
| `profit` on `GET /external/v1/balance` | What your sales have earned so far. |
| `price` in the gift card and eSIM catalogs | Your cost price, all markups above you included. |
