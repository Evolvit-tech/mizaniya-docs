---
title: "Mizaniya Pay Partner API"
description: "Sell mobile top-ups, gift cards and eSIMs from your own website or app."
---

The Mizaniya Pay Partner API lets your own website, app or back office sell what a Mizaniya Pay point of sale sells, using your account's balance:

- **Mobile top-ups** for Djezzy, Ooredoo and Mobilis numbers.
- **Gift cards** for games, streaming and other digital services.
- **eSIMs** for travel, by country, region or worldwide.

Every request is made by your server with an API key, and every sale is paid from your Mizaniya Pay balance at the price your account gets in the app.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an API key">
    Create a key in the developer area of the Mizaniya Pay app and choose what it may do (its scopes). See [Authentication](/authentication).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the catalog">
    List what you can sell and at what price: top-up plans, gift card denominations or eSIM packages.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place the order">
    Send one `POST /external/v1/orders` with the product `type` and its fields. The price is taken from your balance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get the outcome">
    Most orders finish after a short wait. Poll `GET /external/v1/orders/{id}` until the order is final, then deliver the result to your customer.
  </Step>
</Steps>
